MR CRIDDLE

"The Criddle Files" is the Sophomore LP by the legendary experimental ambient introspective jazz duo Mr. Criddle & the Crups. Developed and strategically edited and sanded down to its finest parts over a period of 8 million years, the genius minds of musicians rylsblaze and bckyrdc4mp1ng were able to flawlessly follow up their star-studded debut "Backyard Blaze", pimping out their signature sound to a nothing short of mind-blowing blockbuster.
